Asset-secured loans

Stepwise Ventures will soon offer small loans secured by your valuables. Bring in an item, we assess its value, and you receive a loan based on that value.

How it will work

  1. Bring your valuable. We inspect it and give you a fair valuation.
  2. Receive your loan. The loan amount is based on the assessed value of your item, and we keep the item safe.
  3. Repay and collect. Pay back the loan plus the agreed interest, and your item is returned to you.

Loan terms, interest and repayment periods will be explained and agreed in writing before any loan is made. Details will be announced before launch.
